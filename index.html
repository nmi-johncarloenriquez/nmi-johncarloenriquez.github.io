A little bit about me.

Hi, I’m John Carlo Enriquez, but most people call me Jaycee. I’m currently based in Meycauayan City, Bulacan, and I’ve been working in web development for almost 8 years. My expertise is mainly in PHP, JavaScript, MySQL, APIs, CMS platforms, and modern web technologies. I enjoy building, maintaining, and improving websites and web applications—and yes, I spend a lot of time fixing things that somehow decided to break on a Friday afternoon.

Throughout my career, I’ve had the opportunity to work on projects for companies such as GMA Network, SM Supermalls, Mister Donut, and Avon. I’ve contributed to websites, CMS platforms, internal systems, microsites, and various web applications, while working with different teams and technologies along the way. I graduated from AMA University with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, which gave me the foundation to turn my curiosity about technology into an actual career.

What keeps me going is my passion for learning, problem-solving, and adapting to new technologies. I want to continue growing into a well-rounded and dependable web developer who can contribute beyond simply writing code. When I approach my work, I focus on understanding the problem first, finding a practical solution, and making sure the result is maintainable and useful to the people who will actually use it. I may not know everything—and honestly, nobody does—but I’m always willing to learn, adapt, and figure things out.
